# Operator Uplift — Website

## Role
Marketing site + Privy auth + Solana Pay gate + audit viewer. Owns `operatoruplift.com` and every `/api/*` route the web dashboard talks to. Brand: **commitment infrastructure** — "Keep your word. Bet on yourself." You stake money on a commitment, upload proof, an impartial AI Game Master verifies follow-through, and stakes settle on Solana (honored stakes return; missed stakes redistribute to operators who kept theirs).

> The earlier "AI for your inbox and calendar" assistant pitch was retired in the 2026-05-22 pivot. The web dashboard is a thin shell now: `/goals` is the only live surface; the 14 leftover AI-assistant routes were deleted in the 2026-06-08 prune and their old URLs 308-redirect to `/` (see `next.config.ts`). The mobile-first commitment app lands on iOS + Android.

## Trust-gate
Source-level (`scripts/check.mjs::trust-gate`): every `app/api/*/route.ts` imports `@/lib/apiHelpers` and calls `withRequestMeta`. Currently 72/72 (100%).

Runtime (the spec above): every response — including the middleware's 401 — carries `X-Request-Id`. The middleware mints a fresh `req_<uuid>` if the request doesn't already have one and forwards it to the handler so end-to-end IDs match. Count dropped 74 → 72 in the 2026-06-08 prune when `/api/chat` and `/api/agents` were deleted.
