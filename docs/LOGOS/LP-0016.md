# LP-0016: Anonymous Forum with Threshold Moderation and Membership Revocation

**Status**: Not entered. Prize text captured; design notes only.
**Prize URL**: https://ns.com/LP-0016.md
**Fit**: Tangential. Standalone product; low overlap with the assistant story.

## Prize summary (from Logos Press)

A protocol for anonymous, moderated forums. Members post without revealing their identity and their posts are unlinkable to one another, yet a coordinated group of moderators can act on individual pieces of content, and a member who accumulates too many moderation strikes loses their membership.

## Why this is hard

- **Unlinkability**: each post must be anonymous AND unlinkable to other posts by the same member. Membership proof gets used over and over; the unlinkability has to hold across uses.
- **Threshold moderation**: any single moderator should not be able to act unilaterally. A coordinated group acts together via a private multisig (LP-0002 territory).
- **Strike accumulation**: the protocol has to count strikes against a member without learning which member, which means strikes are tracked via a nullifier scheme.
- **Revocation**: when strikes exceed the threshold, the member's posting privilege is revoked without revealing their identity. New members can replace them.

## What we would build

1. A LEZ program implementing:
   - Member registration (zk proof of valid invite token).
   - Post anonymity (Risc0 proof of "I am a member, I have not been struck out").
   - Moderation action: an LP-0002-style private multisig of moderators flags a post.
   - Strike accumulation: each flag bumps a nullifier-keyed counter.
   - Revocation: when the counter exceeds threshold, the member's nullifier is added to a revocation set.
2. A reference forum client (TypeScript) that lets members post + moderators act + observers read.
3. Tests for: strike accumulation, false-positive recovery, member replacement after revocation.

## Why we are NOT the best-positioned team

- We have no forum product. Building a reference forum to ship this prize means inventing UX from scratch.
- The unlinkability problem is well-studied (Semaphore, BBS+ signatures, RLN) but implementing it cleanly on LEZ + Risc0 is a research-grade exercise.
- LP-0008 + LP-0005 are bigger overlap; spending budget here trades against those.

## What we would NOT do

- Build a production-grade forum frontend.
- Solve search / indexing of forum posts. The prize is about the protocol, not the UX.

## Operator inputs needed before ship
