## Reconstructed scope (from context)

- **Censorship resistance**: documents must remain accessible even when individual nodes go down or operators are pressured.
- **No central server**: storage lives on Logos Storage (or a parallel decentralized layer).
- **No token requirement to publish**: the act of whistleblowing must not require an upfront purchase.
- **Anonymous publishing**: the publisher's identity must not be derivable from the upload.
- **Discoverability**: the document must be findable through an index, not just a content-addressed hash that nobody knows.

## What we would build (rough sketch, pending full prize text)

1. A Logos Basecamp app: web frontend + backing Logos Core module.
2. Document upload flow: zk proof of "I am a human" (some Sybil resistance) without an identity link.
3. Storage backend: Logos Storage with redundancy across nodes.
4. Indexing program: extracts public metadata (title, tags, language) from each upload and exposes a search interface.
5. Anti-spam mechanism: probably proof-of-work or rate-limit by zk proof of "I have not uploaded N times in the last hour."

## Why we are NOT the best-positioned team
